About the barn

Sacred Spa + Wellness opened in August 2022 inside a converted horse barn. The old stalls are now treatment rooms.

The barn sits on a 100-acre horse rehabilitation farm in the countryside outside Saratoga Springs. The spa is open to the public all year.
